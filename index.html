How a night goes

Three steps.
Then just play.

  1. 01

    Add your friends

    Type a name and Joy gives them a colour of their own. Everyone stays saved, and a tap sits someone out for the night.

  2. 02

    Pick a game

    Flick through the deck, choose how long and how bold, and start. Or let Joy surprise you.

  3. Your turn

    03

    Pass the phone around

    Joy says whose turn it is, deals the card, keeps the score, and crowns a winner at the end.

The games

What are we playing?

Eight games, each on a card of its own with an illustration that moves. Turn a card over to see how it plays.

The cards

Pick a deck.
Read it out loud.

About 3,000 cards, written separately in English and Georgian rather than translated. Every deck remembers what it has dealt, so the next night starts with cards nobody has seen.

Tap or swipe for the next one

Is everyone here over 18?

These cards are about flirting, dating, and drinking. We'll only ask once.

Game over

Every winner
gets a title.

Pick how long to play before you start, and a hairline at the top shows how far there is to go. At the end, the winner's card is dealt and turned over, then come the final standings and Play again.

    Just the room

    What Joy doesn't need

    • No account.
    • No sign-in.
    • No ads.
    • No internet.

    Just friends, and one phone

    • Nothing leaves the phone

      Names, colours and settings are kept on the device and nowhere else. Joy has no server to send them to.

    • Plays anywhere

      In the mountains, on a plane, in a basement with no signal. Every card is already on the phone.

    • Nobody watching

      No analytics, no ad SDKs, no trackers. The only people watching the game are the ones playing it.

    • 18+

      18+ asks first

      Before its first card is dealt, the 18+ deck asks once whether everyone is over 18.
